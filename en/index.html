About me

I hold a B.S. in Geophysical Engineering. My quantitative background comes from working with field measurements: checking their quality, detecting anomalies and drawing conclusions from noisy signals. I built on it with TripleTen's Data Science program.

Before working formally in data, I was already using it to solve operational problems in construction and logistics. I forecast material demand and proposed consolidating orders, which cut the unit cost by about 10%. I built a market price reference with web scraping in Python, found purchases far above that reference, and the finding led to a review of purchases in other projects. I also reviewed construction budget assumptions with engineers and architects.

That experience taught me to look at the process behind the numbers and to deliver recommendations that operations teams can actually apply. I want to keep growing in data analysis, Machine Learning and AI applied to business problems.

OilyGiant: where to open 200 oil wells

Python, linear regression, bootstrapping

I predicted the reserves of three regions with linear regression and simulated 1000 explorations with bootstrapping to estimate the profit and risk of each one.

Recommended region: region 1, with 4.8 million USD average profit and a 2% risk of loss.

Telecom: customers about to cancel

Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost

I joined four customer tables, analyzed which profiles cancel the most and compared models with cross-validation inside Pipelines. I tuned XGBoost with GridSearch and adjusted the decision threshold.

ROC AUC of 0.91: it finds 3 out of 4 customers who leave.

Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost
